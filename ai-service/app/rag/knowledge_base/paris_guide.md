# Paris Hyperlocal Insider Travel Guidebook

## Hidden Quarries & Montmartre Artists
Beyond the Louvre queue, explore Place du Tertre in Montmartre early in the morning to see local painters set up easels. Visit the secret Montmartre Vineyard (Clos Montmartre) and enjoy fresh warm croissants from Boulangerie Alexine on Rue Lepic.

## Stained Glass & Covered Passages
Skip long Notre-Dame lines and visit Sainte-Chapelle for stunning 13th-century stained glass windows. Afterwards, stroll through historic 19th-century glass-roofed shopping arcades like Passage des Panoramas and Galerie Vivienne.

## Canal Saint-Martin & Picnic Spot
Join local Parisians for an evening wine and cheese picnic along Canal Saint-Martin. Watch iron footbridges swing open for canal barges, followed by artisan coffee at Ten Belles or craft pastries at Du Pain et des Idées.

## Viewpoints & Vintage Boutiques
For panoramic views of the Eiffel Tower without ticket queues, visit the rooftop terrace of Galeries Lafayette Haussmann or Parc des Buttes-Chaumont. Explore Le Marais for vintage fashion boutiques and authentic falafel at L'As du Fallafel.
