# Goa Coastal Guide & Hidden Beaches

## Secret Beaches & Heritage Walk
- **Kakolem Secret Beach (Tiger Beach)**: Hidden cove surrounded by lush green coconut cliffs with a small natural waterfall cascading directly onto the golden sand. Access via a steep rock staircase.
- **Fontainhas Latin Quarter Heritage Walk**: Walk through Panjim's historic Portuguese quarter between 4:00 PM and 6:00 PM. Admire bright yellow, blue, and terracotta painted heritage homes with classic wooden balconies.
- **Cabo de Rama Fort & Cliff Sunset**: Dramatic red laterite fort ruins in South Goa overlooking panoramic ocean cliffs and untouched coastline.

## Food & Local Secrets
- **Confeitaria 31 De Janeiro**: One of Goa's oldest bakeries in Fontainhas, famous for traditional multi-layered Bebinca, Dodol, and warm mushroom samosas.
- **Fisherman's Wharf & South Goa Beach Shacks**: Try fresh kingfish rava fry, Goan prawn curry rice, and feni cocktails along the tranquil Sal riverbanks.
