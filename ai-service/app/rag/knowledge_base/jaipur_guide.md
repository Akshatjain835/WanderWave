# Jaipur Pink City Guide & Secret Heritage Spots

## Hidden Gems & Architecture
- **Panna Meena Ka Kund Stepwell**: A 16th-century symmetrical geometric stepwell located near Amber Fort. Visit before 9:00 AM for stunning photographs and quiet heritage atmosphere.
- **Nahargarh Fort Sunset Cliff Lounge**: Perched on top of the Aravalli hills, Nahargarh Fort offers 360-degree views over the Pink City illuminated at dusk.
- **Gaitore Ki Chhatriyan**: Royal cenotaphs of Jaipur rulers carved in white marble with delicate domes, situated in a tranquil valley away from city traffic.

## Culinary Secrets & Shopping
- **Lassiwala (MI Road - Paanch Batti)**: Serves rich, creamy kulhad lassi topped with thick malai. Open since 1944; sells out by 3:00 PM every day.
- **Johari Bazaar & Bapu Bazaar**: Historic walled markets for Kundan and Meenakari jewelry, Bandhani tie-dye sarees, handcrafted leather mojris, and Jaipur blue pottery.
