# Rishikesh Hyperlocal Insider Travel Guidebook

## Spiritual Ghats & Ganga Aarti
Attend the mesmerizing evening Ganga Aarti at Triveni Ghat or Parmarth Niketan. Arrive 45 minutes early to secure riverbank seating, listen to Vedic Vedic chanting, and float marigold leaf lamps (diya) down the holy Ganges at dusk.

## Beatles Ashram & Street Murals
Explore Chaurasi Kutia (Beatles Ashram) inside Rajaji Tiger Reserve. Discover historic meditation domes (gufa), colorful graffiti murals painted by global artists, and peaceful forest walking trails overlooking the river.

## White Water Rafting & Cliff Jumping
Experience Grade III & IV white water rafting starting from Shivpuri down to Lakshman Jhula (16 km stretch). Pause mid-river for cliff jumping into refreshing Himalayan river pools and sip piping hot Masala Maggi at riverbank shacks.

## Organic Cafes & Yoga Retreats
Rishikesh is the Yoga Capital of the World. Attend sunrise Hatha Yoga classes at Swarg Ashram, followed by vegan smoothie bowls and wood-fired sourdough pizza at Little Buddha Cafe and Freedom Cafe along the riverbank cliffs.
