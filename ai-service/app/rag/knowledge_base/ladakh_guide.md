# Ladakh Altitude Guide & High Mountain Pass Tips

## Stargazing & High Altitude Spots
- **Hanle Dark Sky Reserve & Stargazing**: World's highest altitude dark sky sanctuary located at 4,500m. Offers crystal clear views of the Milky Way galaxy and astronomical telescopes.
- **Pangong Tso & Chang La Pass**: Pristine high-altitude salt lake changing colors from turquoise to deep blue. Access via Chang La Pass (5,360m).

## Health & Altitude Safety Rules
- **48-Hour Mandatory AMS Acclimatization**: Rest completely for 48 hours upon landing in Leh (3,500m) before traveling to higher passes (Khardung La, Pangong). Drink plenty of water and carry Diamox or oxygen cylinders.
