# Manali Alpine Guide & Himalayan Trails

## Hidden Spots & Adventure
- **Jogini Waterfalls Pine Orchard Trail**: Scenic 3km nature trek starting from Vashisht village through pine forests and apple orchards, ending at a cascading double waterfall.
- **Sethan Igloo Village & Igloo Camping**: Located 45 minutes above Manali at 2,700m in Sethan Valley. Famous for snow camping, bouldering, and winter igloo stays.
- **Atal Tunnel & Sissu Lahaul Valley Excursion**: Drive through the 9.02km Atal engineering tunnel into Lahaul valley to visit the grand Sissu waterfall and snow parks.

## Local Cafes & Dining
- **Old Manali Riverside Cafes**: Enjoy wood-fired thin crust pizzas, fresh Himalayan trout fish, and organic mountain teas accompanied by acoustic live guitar performances.
- **Vashisht Hot Sulfur Springs**: Natural therapeutic thermal baths next to an ancient stone temple. Visit early morning for relaxing warm soak.
