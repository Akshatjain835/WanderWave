# Mysore Local Travel Guide & Hidden Spots

## Hidden Gems & Unique Spots
- **Balmuri Check Dam & Cauvery Falls**: Located 15km from Mysore city. Best visited early morning at 7:00 AM to enjoy calm waters, hot fried fresh river fish, and serene nature walks before tourist buses arrive.
- **Chamundi Hill 1,000 Stone Steps Walk**: A historic granite step climb starting from the foot of Chamundi Hill. Ascend at 6:00 AM for fresh mountain air and sweeping sunrise views over Mysore city.
- **Karanji Lake Walk-Through Aviary**: India's largest walk-through aviary housing peacocks, hornbills, and rare migratory birds. Features pedal boating and lush lakeside bamboo gardens.

## Culinary & Local Dining Secrets
- **Gayathri Tiffin Room (GTR)**: Famous for butter-dripping Mysore Masala Dosa and fresh hot melt-in-mouth Mysore Pak served daily at 4:30 PM.
- **Mylari Dosa (Agrahara)**: Tiny legendary eatery open since 1938 serving soft, airy white dosas with coconut chutney and spiced potato filling.
- **Devaraja Spice & Flower Market**: 100-year-old heritage market. Best for purchasing pure sandalwood oil, handmade incense sticks, and vibrant kumkum powders.
