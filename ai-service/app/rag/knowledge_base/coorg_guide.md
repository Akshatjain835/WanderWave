# Coorg (Kodagu) Hyperlocal Insider Travel Guidebook

## Coffee Estate Trails & Homestays
Stay at a traditional Kodava homestay inside a working Arabica and Robusta coffee plantation in Madikeri or Pollibetta. Take guided morning plantation walks to learn about pepper vine inter-cropping, cardamom harvesting, and fresh coffee bean processing.

## Waterfalls & Scenic Viewpoints
Visit Abbey Falls early in the morning around 8:00 AM before tour buses arrive. For breathtaking panoramic views of the Western Ghats, watch the sunset from Raja's Seat gardens or hike up Mandalpatti peak via rugged 4x4 Jeep trails.

## Tibetan Culture at Bylakuppe
Visit Namdroling Nyingmapa Monastery (Golden Temple) in Bylakuppe, one of the largest Tibetan settlements in South India. Admire 40-foot gilded Buddha statues, listen to monastic prayer chants, and sample authentic Tibetan Thukpa and steamed Momos.

## Wildlife & Elephant Interaction
Visit Dubare Elephant Camp along the banks of the Kaveri River. Participate in morning elephant bathing and feeding sessions guided by forest department mahouts, followed by river rafting down calm rapids.
